Global Energy Infrastructure Map

An interactive map of 1000+ global energy assets; refineries, storage, ports, production fields, and logistics routes built from market research into a single tool for understanding oil flows.

Why I built this:

I was reading market intelligence and trade press on oil flows and geopolitical risk but kept losing track of how it all fit together spatially. This map turns that scattered reading into something I can actually reason with, and reveals how physical constraints translate into market risk.

What it shows:

1000+ assets across the full value chain: production fields, refineries, storage, ports/terminals, and logistics routes
Structured profiles per asset (crude grade/benchmark, operator, export route, current risk factors)
Physical and financial price assessments tied to each asset, not just location
Descriptions enriched with live market context 

Features:

Filter by category and region
Search by location
Toggle layers and transport routes on/off
Click a marker for the full asset profile

Built with: Python · Streamlit · Google Earth Pro / KML
